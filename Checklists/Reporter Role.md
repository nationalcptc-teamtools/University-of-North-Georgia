# CPTC Captain/Reporter Role

**My Job:** Keep everyone on task, keep the chaos organized, make sure we have a report, and TURN GEEK SPEAK INTO EXECUTIVE SPEAK.

## Starting the Chaos

- Review scope and rules (we are NOT getting disqualified)
    
- Make sure everyone is alive, awake, and ready
    
- Assign initial targets
    
- Make sure everyone knows what they should be doing
    
- Get the report and findings tracker open
    
- Remind everyone: DOCUMENT AND SCREENSHOT EVERYTHING
    

##  Herding the Hackers

- Know what everyone is working on
    
- Check in with everyone periodically
    
- Make sure we aren't all attacking the same thing
    
- Redirect people when they're stuck (no spending 3 hours on one rabbit hole)
    
- Reassign targets when necessary
    
- Make sure nobody is going rogue or out of scope
    
- Make sure people are actually communicating their findings
    
- Send Floater wherever the chaos is greatest
    
- Keep an eye on the clock (time is NOT our friend)
    

##  DOCUMENT DOCUMENT DOCUMENT

- Review findings as they come in
    
- Make sure every finding has screenshots/evidence
    
- Make sure findings are actually making it into the report
    
- Check that technical details make sense
    
- Make sure we aren't reporting the same vulnerability 47 times
    
- Assign severity ratings
    
- Connect vulnerabilities into attack chains
    
- Make sure we explain WHY the vulnerability matters
    
- Ensure remediation recommendations are included
    
- Hunt down anyone who has findings but hasn't documented them (politely or maybe not politely)
    

##  Turning Geek Speak Into Executive Speak

- Keep the report updated throughout the competition
    
- Start building the executive summary EARLY
    
- Identify our biggest findings and risks
    
- Translate technical details into understandable language
    
- Explain how vulnerabilities could actually impact the client
    
- Make sure the report tells a story instead of being a giant vulnerability dump
    
- Check for missing sections or evidence
    
- Make sure the report looks like it was written by one team, not six sleep-deprived caffeine fueled college kids
    

##  Injects & Client Interactions

- Read the inject carefully (ALL OF IT)
    
- Figure out what they're actually asking us to do
    
- Assign tasks if the inject requires technical work
    
- Keep track of inject deadlines
    
- Get the necessary information from teammates
    
- Make sure responses are professional and accurate
    
- IF YOU DON'T KNOW WHAT IS HAPPENING, DON'T TALK
    
- Don't make promises or claims we can't back up
    
- Submit inject responses on time
    
- Document important client communications
    

##  The Final Countdown

- Tell everyone to STOP HACKING AND START WRITING
    
- Make sure all findings have been submitted
    
- Check for missing screenshots and evidence
    
- Remove duplicate findings
    
- Verify severity ratings and remediation recommendations
    
- Finish the executive summary
    
- Proofread the entire report
    
- Make sure the report is technically accurate AND understandable
    
- Check formatting (we are professionals, allegedly)
    
- Verify submission requirements
    
- SUBMIT THE REPORT ON TIME
    
- Celebrate surviving CPTC 🎉
    

**GOAL: Find cool stuff. Document cool stuff. Explain why the cool stuff is**
**Side Goal**: Bring everyone back alive
